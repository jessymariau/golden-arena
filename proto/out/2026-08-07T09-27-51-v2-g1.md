## The table

```
GPT       wildcard  N1 N2 E1     one from NORTH: needs N3, held by GEMINI
HAIKU     diplomat  E2 E3 S1     one from EAST: needs E1, held by GPT
GEMINI    shark     N3 W1 S2     —
DEEPSEEK  wildcard  W2 W3 S3     one from WEST: needs W1, held by GEMINI
```

Regions: NORTH = N1 N2 N3 · EAST = E1 E2 E3 · SOUTH = S1 S2 S3 · WEST = W1 W2 W3

Nobody starts with a complete region. The seats are deliberately unequal. Land moves by agreement, or by all three others raiding the same person at once.

`INVEST +20 · FORTIFY · RAID` — 2+ raiders take 40 each; a lone raider fails for −15 **unless the target is MARKED**; all three take a territory instead of coins. Breaking a handshake, a raid pledge or a fortify pledge marks you in public for two turns.
